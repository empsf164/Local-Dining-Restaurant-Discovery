About the Bakery

Maison Crème is a sanctuary of classic French viennoiserie and avant-garde plated entremets. Hand-laminated 72-layer croissants, organic Madagascar vanilla bean mille-feuille, and curated single-origin roasts in an elegant sunlit brass and marble space.

The Artisan Story

Founded by Master Pastry Chef Julien Laurent after a decade at Paris's top Michelin pastry laboratories, Maison Crème brings meticulous artisanal lamination technique and uncompromised butter quality to our historic arts district.
